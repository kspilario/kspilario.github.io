### COURSE DESCRIPTION
Total system design. Systems theory and application of modeling and simulation techniques in energy systems design. Optimization models and tools for energy systems.

### COURSE CREDIT
3 units (3 hrs/week)

### COURSE OBJECTIVES
After completing this course, the student should be able to:
* Develop dynamic models of energy systems to analyze feedback structures, delays, and long-term behavior.
* Solve energy optimization problems using linear, mixed-integer, and nonlinear programming.
* Evaluate the assumptions, boundaries, and limitations of dynamical system models and optimization algorithms in the context of energy engineering.
* Communicate the results of modeling studies in a clear, concise manner through technical reports and presentations.
